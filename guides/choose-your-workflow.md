# Choose your workflow

Choose the workflow that matches what you need to do now. The workflows complement one another; a useful sequence is to learn a topic, retrieve it from memory, then practise applying it in an exam-style answer.

| Need | Workflow | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Build understanding of unfamiliar or difficult material | [learn-topic](../skills/learn-topic/SKILL.md) | The AI explains progressively, checks comprehension one question at a time, and adapts to your answers. |
| Find out what you can recall and apply without seeing an answer first | [active-recall](../skills/active-recall/SKILL.md) | The AI asks one question at a time, gives feedback, revisits weak concepts, and ends with a revision summary. |
| Improve a draft response to an exam question | [exam-marker](../skills/exam-marker/SKILL.md) | The AI compares your response with the question and any supplied rubric, then gives reasoned feedback and a model answer. |

## A practical sequence

1. Use `learn-topic` when the concept is unclear or prerequisites are missing.
2. Use `active-recall` after studying, without looking at your notes while answering.
3. Use `exam-marker` with an authorised practice question and your own attempt. Include the official marking guide when available.
4. Use the resulting weak points to choose the next topic or question.

You can start with any workflow. If you have no notes or the source is unreadable, ask the AI to say what it can and cannot access before proceeding. If there is no official rubric, treat any mark as an estimate rather than an institutional result.
