# Privacy and limitations

StudySuperpowers is a set of written workflows, not a hosting service. When you use an AI project, the platform provider processes the files and conversation you submit under its own terms, settings, retention practices, and account or workspace controls.

## Before uploading

- Check your institution's policy and the platform's current privacy and data controls.
- Upload only course materials you have permission to share with that service. Copyright and course-access rules still apply to personal study use.
- Do not upload personal information about classmates, student records, unpublished research, exam materials you are not authorised to use, or confidential course content.
- Consider whether project sharing is enabled. Anyone with access to a shared project may be able to see its files and conversations, depending on the platform.
- Review project settings and remove materials you no longer need. Deleting a project or a file may have platform-specific effects; consult its current help documentation.

## Academic integrity

Use the workflows to practise and learn, not to misrepresent authorship. Follow your university's rules for AI assistance in assignments and examinations. Do not use a model during a restricted assessment, and do not submit generated explanations or answers as your own where that is prohibited. When uncertain, ask your instructor.

## Model limitations

AI systems may misread files, overlook details, mix up conflicting versions, or state plausible but unsupported claims. Ask the AI to name the source it used. Verify definitions, calculations, and high-stakes advice against the official course materials or an instructor.

The `exam-marker` workflow provides feedback for practice. It cannot certify a grade. A mark based on an AI-generated rubric or incomplete evidence is an estimate, and even rubric-based feedback may differ from an instructor's judgement.
